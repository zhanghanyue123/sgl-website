## Sales, Shipping & Returns — /shipping

**Metadata title**  
Sales, Shipping & Returns — The Seagull Labs

**Metadata description**  
Read SGL ordering and delivery information: USD pricing, stock confirmation, shipping enquiries, Station pickup and support with returns or damaged items.

**Introduction**  
Planning an order? Here is what to check with the SGL team, from product availability to delivery and after-sales support.

**Ordering & Availability**  
For an order enquiry, email the product name, size or colour, quantity and destination to hello@theseagulllabs.com. Confirm current stock and payment arrangements with SGL before paying. Adding a product to the cart does not reserve stock.

**Prices & Payment**  
Website shop prices are displayed in US dollars. International orders use secure card checkout via Stripe when online checkout is enabled. Mainland China orders support WeChat Pay and Alipay through direct confirmation with SGL; these methods are not currently integrated as website checkout buttons. Confirm the final total, payment currency, delivery charge and any applicable taxes before paying.

**Delivery Destinations & Charges**  
Contact SGL with your country, city and postal code to check delivery availability. Shipping charges depend on destination and parcel. Request a quote before ordering.

**Dispatch & Delivery Times**  
Ask SGL for a dispatch estimate and available shipping service when confirming the order. Confirm timing before ordering for a specific date.

**International Orders**  
For overseas delivery, confirm which taxes or import charges are included in the quote and which, if any, are payable on arrival.

**Station Pickup**  
Ask whether the selected item can be collected at Seagull Station in Shanghai. Confirm stock, the collection address and a pickup time before visiting.

**Returns, Exchanges & Support**  
Request the applicable return and exchange terms before purchasing. For a return, exchange, cancellation, incorrect item or damage, email the order reference and a description of the issue. Include photographs of damage where available and request instructions before sending anything back.
