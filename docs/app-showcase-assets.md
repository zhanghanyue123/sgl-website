# App showcase image localization

Created with the built-in imagegen editor from five user-provided screenshots. These are marketing previews, not live app screens. Interface text was localized to English, retaining the cream/black/yellow design and original illustration themes. Map artwork is illustrative and must not be used for navigation. Menu amounts retain CNY; they are not USD store prices.

Published assets in `public/app/`:
- `map-en.webp`: station map, first stop and friends feed.
- `refuel-en.webp`: drink menu.
- `goods-en.webp`: coffee and membership pricing.
- `missions-en.webp`: daily missions.
- `story-en.webp`: illustrated city adventure.
